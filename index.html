How to read the metrics
▲ Higher is better Impact Factor · CiteScore · SJR · h5-index — larger values signal greater reach, visibility and influence.
▼ Lower is better Acceptance rate — a lower figure means the venue is more selective. Difficulty is a qualitative rating: higher difficulty = harder to publish.
Tiers, quartiles & deadlines
Quartile = best SCImago (SJR) category quartile; Q1 = top 25% of journals in that category. JCR quartiles (Web of Science) can differ.
Deadlines are full-paper deadlines, Anywhere on Earth (UTC−12). est. = projected from the previous edition, not yet announced. When a deadline passes, the card rolls over automatically to the next edition with a projected date (dashed border) until you enter the official one. Countdown: ≤14 days≤45 dayslater
